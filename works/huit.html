HUIT

Type French restaurant
Year 2026
Location Fukuoka, Japan

The time spent waiting for a dish becomes part of the meal. Counter seating brings diners close to the life of the kitchen, while a private room offers a quieter setting. Warm wood and soft light within a palette of deep tones create a space where attention turns naturally to food and conversation.

料理を待つ時間も、食事の一部になる。厨房の気配を身近に感じるカウンターと、静かに過ごせる個室。深い色調のなかに木の温もりと柔らかな光を配し、料理と会話に自然と気持ちが向く空間を目指した。
